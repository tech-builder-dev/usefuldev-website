Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how UsefulDev handles information in connection with Sprint Readiness for Jira.

Information processed by the app

Sprint Readiness for Jira processes Jira data as necessary to perform sprint readiness checks requested by users. This can include issue fields and related Jira metadata required to evaluate the configured readiness checks.

Data storage

UsefulDev does not operate an external database or external backend for Sprint Readiness for Jira.

Jira issue content and sprint scan results are processed on demand and are not persisted by UsefulDev in an external system.

Project-level application settings are stored using Atlassian Forge storage. These settings may include configuration such as enabled checks and Jira field, status, issue type, or issue-link identifiers selected for those checks.

External data transfers

Sprint Readiness for Jira does not send Jira issue content to an external UsefulDev server or third-party analytics service.

Analytics and tracking

Sprint Readiness for Jira does not include third-party advertising trackers or third-party product analytics services.

Support communications

If you contact UsefulDev for support, we receive the information that you voluntarily provide in that communication, such as your email address and the contents of your support request. This information is used to respond to and resolve your request.

Atlassian

Sprint Readiness for Jira is built using Atlassian Forge and interacts with Atlassian services. Atlassian's handling of information is governed by Atlassian's own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to support@usefuldev.dev.
